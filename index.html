Got an invention idea?

File a patent disclosure

Three steps to get your idea in front of Walmart's patent committee.

  1. 1

    Write it up with the template

    Use the Supplemental Invention Disclosure Form to draft your idea.

    ⚠ Don't use Gen AI or agents to draft the document. Inventive aspects get buried in wordiness, and poor prompting can create legal issues. Use Gen AI only to review for readability, clarity, conciseness, and missing technical details.

  2. 2

    Get a committee sponsor

    Pick a sponsor from the Patent Committee Roster — an informal meeting to get candid feedback (clarity, novelty emphasis, missing detail). Add their name to the top of page 1 of your form.

    💡 The Operations patent committee covers central ops and has more bandwidth than Global Tech — best fit for Chapa's org.

  3. 3

    Submit through the portal

    Copy & paste your write-up into the Anaqua submission form.

    It'll be scheduled into a monthly committee meeting. The Operations committee also gives you a live presentation slot. A presentation template will be sent once your disclosure is received.
